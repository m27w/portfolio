Marlét Clo

Luxury streetwear — Capsule 002 live at marletclo.com

Type E-Commerce Store
Platform Shopify
Role Full-Stack + Design
Live At marletclo.com →

Overview

The Brief

Marlét Clo is a luxury streetwear brand built around limited capsules — designed once, produced in limited numbers, and never restocked once they sell through. The brief was a dark, minimal Shopify store that felt premium from the first scroll, with the infrastructure to launch and sell each drop.

The live store at marletclo.com now runs Capsule 002 (two shirts, bundle pricing) alongside Capsule 001 Essentials. The build covered the full stack: bespoke theme, product architecture, DNS/email deliverability, and Meta Ads to drive traffic into the drop.

Approach

How It Came Together

The brand asked for a dark, minimal storefront that felt premium from the first scroll — so the Shopify theme, typography and colour system were built around that identity rather than a stock template.

Launch meant more than a product grid. Store, email authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), and Meta Ads were treated as one stack so each capsule could go live with checkout, inbox deliverability and paid social already in place.

The store now sells Capsule 002 with bundle pricing (get both shirts for £60) and keeps Capsule 001 Essentials in the permanent collection — still never restocked, designed in the UK, with free UK shipping and 14-day returns.

Scope

What I Built

01

Shopify Store Build

Built the full Shopify store from scratch — product setup, collection architecture, checkout flow, and a bespoke theme customisation to match the brand's dark, minimal aesthetic. Typography, spacing and colour decisions were made to position the brand at the premium end of the market.

Shopify Liquid CSS UI Design
02

Email Deliverability & DNS

Configured full DNS authentication for transactional and marketing email — SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records set up and verified to ensure order confirmations and campaigns land in the inbox rather than spam. Resolved existing deliverability issues that were affecting customer communication.

DNS SPF / DKIM / DMARC Email Deliverability
03

Meta Ads Setup & Management

Set up and actively managed Meta Ads campaigns targeting GB, IE, and US markets. Instagram-only placements with CBO budget structure, optimised for purchase events. Ongoing creative direction, audience refinement, and performance analysis to drive traffic to the drop.

Meta Ads Instagram CBO Paid Social
04

Conversion Optimisation

Investigated and addressed abandoned checkout issues — analysing drop-off points, reviewing checkout UX, and implementing fixes to recover lost conversions. Ongoing discount logic management and promotional mechanics on Shopify.

CRO Shopify Analytics
