Overview

What I'm Building

Strand is my own product — a training OS for hybrid athletes who lift, run, swim and track nutrition in the same week. Most apps do one discipline well and ignore the rest. Strand keeps everything in one log and surfaces conflicts between them before they cost you.

The marketing site and product UI are live at mystrand.app while the full launch rolls out. Starter is free; Plus and Pro unlock deeper logging, social and analytics.

Product

One Log. Real Training.

Home dashboard, session logging, nutrition tracking, friends, leaderboards and profile stats — designed as one system so hybrid training doesn't get split across four apps.
