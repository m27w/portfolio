Marlét Clo

Luxury streetwear — debut headwear capsule drop

Type E-Commerce Store
Platform Shopify
Role Full-Stack + Design
Live At marletclo.com →

Overview

The Brief

Marlét Clo is a luxury streetwear brand launching its debut headwear capsule. The brief was to build a Shopify store that matched the brand's identity — dark, minimal, high-end — and set up the infrastructure needed to launch and market the collection from day one.

The project covered the full stack: store build, bespoke theme customisation, email marketing setup, DNS and deliverability configuration, and a live Meta Ads operation to drive traffic to the drop.

Scope

What I Built

01

Shopify Store Build

Built the full Shopify store from scratch — product setup, collection architecture, checkout flow, and a bespoke theme customisation to match the brand's dark, minimal aesthetic. Typography, spacing and colour decisions were made to position the brand at the premium end of the market.

Shopify Liquid CSS UI Design
02

Email Deliverability & DNS

Configured full DNS authentication for transactional and marketing email — SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records set up and verified to ensure order confirmations and campaigns land in the inbox rather than spam. Resolved existing deliverability issues that were affecting customer communication.

DNS SPF / DKIM / DMARC Email Deliverability
03

Meta Ads Setup & Management

Set up and actively managed Meta Ads campaigns targeting GB, IE, and US markets. Instagram-only placements with CBO budget structure, optimised for purchase events. Ongoing creative direction, audience refinement, and performance analysis to drive traffic to the drop.

Meta Ads Instagram CBO Paid Social
04

Conversion Optimisation

Investigated and addressed abandoned checkout issues — analysing drop-off points, reviewing checkout UX, and implementing fixes to recover lost conversions. Ongoing discount logic management and promotional mechanics on Shopify.

CRO Shopify Analytics
